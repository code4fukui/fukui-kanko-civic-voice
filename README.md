# fukui-kanko-civic-voice

> 日本語のREADMEはこちらです: [README.ja.md](README.ja.md)

This repository contains survey data and visualizations exploring the impact of tourism on the well-being and happiness of residents in Fukui Prefecture, Japan. The data is collected periodically to inform tourism promotion policies.

## Demo

- **[Interactive Survey Results](https://code4fukui.github.io/fukui-kanko-civic-voice/#kanko_202408.csv)**: An interactive pie chart and table viewer for the survey data. Use the dropdown to select different questions.
- **[Split-Screen Comparison](https://code4fukui.github.io/fukui-kanko-civic-voice/split.html)**: A two-panel view to compare different datasets or questions side-by-side.
- **[Three-Screen Comparison](https://code4fukui.github.io/fukui-kanko-civic-voice/split3.html)**: A three-panel view for more extensive comparative analysis.

## Features

- **Interactive Chart Generator**: Visualize survey results with dynamic pie charts and tables.
- **Data Filtering**: Select different survey questions from a dropdown menu to update the visualization in real-time.
- **Comparative Analysis**: Use the split-screen and three-screen HTML pages to load multiple instances of the chart generator for side-by-side comparison.

## Data

The following survey result files are available in this repository:

- [kanko_20240117-1.csv](kanko_20240117-1.csv) - Survey results from January 2024
- [kanko_202408.csv](kanko_202408.csv) - Survey results from August 2024
- [kanko_20250119.csv](kanko_20250119.csv) - Survey results from January 2025
- [kanko_202508.csv](kanko_202508.csv) - Survey results from August 2025
- [kanko_202601.csv](kanko_202601.csv) - Survey results from January 2026

The data is provided by the [Fukui Prefecture Tourism DX Promotion Marketing Data Consortium](https://www.fukuishimbun.co.jp/category/fukui-kankodx) and is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
